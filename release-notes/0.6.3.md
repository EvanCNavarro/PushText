# 0.6.3

## Fixed

- **A long dictation is no longer thrown away.** Speaking for more than two minutes used to end the
  recording and discard everything you had said - no text, no history entry, no explanation. The
  words are kept now: the recording is transcribed and inserted as normal.

- **You can talk for twenty minutes.** The old two-minute ceiling was far too short for the
  hands-free mode, which is exactly the mode that invites talking at length.

- **If a recording does hit the limit, PushText says so.** The transcript is marked as stopped at the
  limit, rather than simply ending mid-sentence and leaving you to wonder what happened.
