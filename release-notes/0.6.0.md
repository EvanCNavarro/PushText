# 0.6.0

## New

- **Silence other audio while you dictate.** A switch under **Sound**. PushText mutes the Mac while
  you are speaking and puts the volume back exactly as it was when you stop - including if you
  cancel, and including if the app is killed mid-sentence, which it repairs the next time it starts.

  Off by default. On a laptop the speakers sit inches from the microphone, so whatever is playing
  ends up in the recording; turn this on if that is happening to you.

## Improved

- **The Globe-key notice says less, and what it says is true.** It used to claim macOS acts "when you
  press Globe, and it acts first". macOS may be set to act on a DOUBLE press, and whether it wins the
  race has not been measured - so the notice now reports only what System Settings shows.
