# 0.3.1

## Improved

- **Searching your history forgives a typo.** Looking for a dictation about an invoice and typing
  "invoce" now finds it. Longer words forgive more; short ones are still matched exactly, so
  searching for "car" does not hand you every dictation mentioning a cat.

- **Matches are highlighted where they are.** The words that matched are marked in the transcript,
  so on a long dictation you can see straight away which part you were looking for - including when
  what you typed and what you said are not spelled the same.

Typing a second word narrows the results rather than widening them, and results stay in the order
you recorded them.
