# 0.2.2

Two things you can see, both in the menu.

## New

- **PushText tells you when an update is waiting.** A small amber dot appears on the menu-bar icon,
  on the `...` button and on "Check for Updates". Nothing opens on its own and there is no dialog to
  dismiss - the dot appears only when there is genuinely something to install, never while PushText
  is merely checking.
- **A missing permission now comes with a button.** The Accessibility notice used to be a sentence
  telling you where to look in System Settings. It is a fix-it row now, and it takes you there. When
  the permission was granted before and stopped working, it clears the stale entry first - an entry
  left over from an earlier version of PushText cannot be switched back on, so removing it is the
  only thing that lets this copy be allowed.

## Arrived in 0.2.1 and never announced

0.2.1 was a crash fix and its notes said only that, so these went out unmentioned:

- **Set the dictation key by pressing it.** The Hotkey control is a field you click - it says
  "Press a key...", and you press the one you want. Right Option, Right Command, Right Control,
  Right Shift or Left Option.
- **The menu no longer says "State: Ready".** That row appears only when there is something to
  report, such as a permission problem or a model still downloading.
