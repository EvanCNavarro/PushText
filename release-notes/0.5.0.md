# 0.5.0

## New

- **Launch at login.** A switch under **General** in the menu. PushText starts when you log in, so a
  hold-to-talk key is there without you going to find it first.

  macOS may ask you to approve it the first time, under System Settings, General, Login Items - the
  menu says so when that is what it is waiting for, rather than showing the switch on while nothing
  happens.

  Uninstalling removes the login item too.
