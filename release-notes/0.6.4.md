# 0.6.4

## Fixed

- **Dictation History updates while it is open.** Leaving the window up and recording something new
  used to show nothing - the list was read once when the window opened and never again, so a
  transcript you had just spoken was missing from the history that was meant to hold it. New
  dictations now appear at the top the moment they are saved.

- **Deleting your history clears the window you are looking at.** It used to keep showing transcripts
  that were already gone from disk.
