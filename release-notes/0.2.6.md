# 0.2.6

## Fixed

- **Uninstall now removes your data, not just the app.** It used to move PushText to the Trash and
  leave everything else behind - your dictation history, your custom dictionary, the settings, the
  caches, and the Microphone and Accessibility entries in System Settings. All of it goes now, and
  the confirmation says exactly what will be removed.

  Everything is moved to the **Trash** rather than deleted, so an uninstall you regret is
  recoverable.
